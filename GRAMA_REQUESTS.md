# GRAMA Request Drafts — UVU Security Records

**Drafted:** August 2026
**Purpose:** Obtain records bearing on the pre-event security posture at Utah Valley University for the September 10, 2025 event, targeting the specific gaps logged at contradictions X8, X30 and X46.

> **Verify before filing.** Statutory subsection numbers below should be checked against current Utah Code Title 63G Chapter 2. The subsections attributed to UVU are the ones UVU itself cited in its prior release, not independent legal conclusions. Nothing here is legal advice.

---

## Why the previous requests failed

Two efforts are on the public record and both failed structurally, not randomly:

| Requester | Asked for | Outcome | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fox 13 | The event security plan | Denied; state records director ruled against | The request sits *inside* the security-records exemption on its face. There was no version of it that could succeed. |
| Daily Caller | Chief Long's communications, broadly | Released, then gutted | 682 redactions across 96 of 113 pages. 538 privacy redactions, ~302 of them names in a single group chat. Breadth invites mass redaction. |

**The lesson:** operational specificity triggers the security exemption; breadth triggers the privacy exemption. A request has to thread between them.

**File these as six separate requests, not one bundle.** A single request containing one clearly exempt item invites a global denial. Separate requests force separate written determinations, and each denial becomes independently appealable.

## Target 1 — Orem Police Department *(highest value, never attempted)*

**Custodian: Orem PD — NOT UVU.** This is the whole point.

Brian Harpole has stated publicly that Orem PD operates an active drone unit, that a mutual-aid agreement exists, and that Orem was never asked to assist. Orem PD holds its own side of that correspondence. UVU cannot redact another agency's records, and Orem PD carries no comparable civil exposure from the event.

**Request:**
> All communications between the Orem Police Department and Utah Valley University or the UVU Police Department, dated August 1, 2025 through September 10, 2025, concerning the September 10, 2025 event at Utah Valley University — including any request for mutual aid, any request for unmanned-aircraft support, any request for supplemental officers, and any response to such a request.
>
> If no such record exists, please state that in writing.

**Why it survives:** the security exemption protects existing records that reveal vulnerabilities. It cannot protect a record that does not exist. A written "no responsive records" answer is itself the finding — and it is the single cheapest unexploited move in this case.

## Target 2 — The mutual-aid agreements

**Custodians: Orem PD and Utah County Sheriff's Office** (file with both; also file with UVU as a cross-check).

**Request:**
> The current mutual-aid agreement, memorandum of understanding, or interlocal cooperation agreement between this agency and Utah Valley University or the UVU Police Department, together with any amendment in effect on September 10, 2025.

**Why it survives:** interagency agreements are ordinarily public administrative records. They are not operational security plans. This establishes what assistance was available for the asking, which is the necessary predicate for Target 1 to mean anything.

## Target 3 — Chief Long / Harpole texts *(the invited request)*

**Custodian: UVU.** Expect a denial. File it anyway — the appeal is the point.

**Request:**
> All text messages, SMS, or other electronic messages exchanged between UVU Police Chief Jeff Long and Brian Harpole between September 1, 2025 and September 10, 2025 inclusive.

**Pre-rebuttal to include in the request itself:**

1. **On privacy (the subsection cited for 538 redactions in your prior release):** Mr. Harpole has publicly and repeatedly invited scrutiny of this exchange, waiving any personal privacy interest on his side. Chief Long is a public officer and these are communications about official duties. The privacy interest that justified redacting ~302 names in a group chat has no application here.

2. **On security records (the subsection cited for 82 content redactions):** that exemption protects against disclosure that *would jeopardize* security. The rooftop access route at issue was exploited on September 10, 2025 and has since been described in globally distributed reporting, in sworn testimony at a public preliminary hearing, and in the charging documents. Withholding a warning about a vulnerability that is already exhaustively public serves no protective purpose the statute recognizes. The prospective harm the exemption exists to prevent has already occurred and cannot be prevented by continued withholding.

3. **On segregation:** if any portion is properly withheld, GRAMA contemplates release of the remainder. Please provide the non-exempt portions rather than withholding the records in full.

## Target 4 — The independent security review *contract*

**Custodian: UVU.**

Note carefully: this does **not** request the report or its findings. It requests the procurement file.

**Request:**
> The contract, purchase order, engagement letter, or statement of work between Utah Valley University and any outside firm retained to conduct the independent review of security at the September 10, 2025 event; all amendments and change orders; the deliverable schedule; and any record of delivery, receipt, acceptance, extension, suspension or termination.

**Why it survives:** procurement records are routinely public. This establishes whether the review was delivered and is being withheld, is overdue, was extended, or was quietly cancelled — a fact currently unknown to the public and logged at **X46**. Those four possibilities have very different meanings and no one outside UVU can presently tell them apart.

## Target 5 — The unexplained drone

**Custodian: UVU** (and separately: FAA, via federal FOIA, for any UAS authorization over the campus that morning).

**Request:**
> Any record identifying the operator of an unmanned aircraft flown over or near the Utah Valley University Orem campus on September 10, 2025, including any authorization, permit, waiver, notification, flight log, complaint, or incident report concerning unmanned aircraft activity on that date.

**Why it survives:** Harpole has stated an unaccounted-for drone was airborne and his team could not identify the operator. The identity of an aircraft operator over a public campus is not an operational security vulnerability of the university. If UVU's own records cannot identify the operator, that answer is as informative as the record would be.

## Target 6 — The redaction log

**Custodian: UVU.**

**Request:**
> The redaction log, index, or withholding schedule prepared in connection with the prior release of records concerning the September 10, 2025 event, identifying each redaction by document, by location, and by the statutory subsection claimed — without the redacted content itself.

**Why it survives:** a log describes *what was withheld and on what basis*. It does not contain the withheld material. An agency asserting an exemption must be able to identify what it is asserting it over. This converts 682 undifferentiated black boxes into a structured map of what exists, and it materially strengthens any subsequent appeal.

## Procedural provisions to include in every request

- **Segregation.** Ask expressly for release of non-exempt portions rather than blanket denial.
- **Written determination.** Request the specific statutory subsection for each withholding, in writing.
- **Fee waiver.** Request one on public-interest grounds; the subject matter is of demonstrated public concern.
- **Expedited response** if you qualify as a member of the media.
- **Existence statement.** Where no responsive record exists, ask for that in writing. Negative answers are findings.

## Expect denial on Targets 3 and 5 — prepare the appeal first

Appeal deadlines under GRAMA are short. Draft the appeal before the denial arrives.

The appeal path runs to the agency's chief administrative officer, then to the **State Records Committee**. The Committee stage matters disproportionately: it is where the public-interest balancing test becomes available, meaning the Committee can order release of a *protected* record where the public interest favoring access outweighs the interest favoring restriction.

That is the only forum in which the central argument — *that a vulnerability already exploited and globally known no longer needs protecting* — can actually be heard. An initial denial is a step in the process, not the end of it.

---

## What this cannot do

None of these requests will establish anything about who fired the shot. They bear on one question only: what UVU knew about its security posture before September 10 and what it chose to do about it. That question is worth pursuing on its own evidence — it is the strongest-supported finding in this case at 96–99% confidence — and it is weakened, not strengthened, by being bundled with theories that have repeatedly failed testing.
